One-Pot Orzo and Chicken Meatballs Recipe

Description

Tender chicken meatballs paired with delicate orzo pasta in a comforting, flavor-packed dish. Juicy, herb-seasoned meatballs are served over light, perfectly cooked orzo that soaks up every savory bite. Finished with fresh herbs, garlic, Parmesan, and a touch of lemon for brightness, this satisfying meal is hearty enough for dinner while remaining fresh and delicious.

Ingredients

  • 1 Pound Ground Chicken
  • 1 Cup Grated Parmesan Cheese, Divided
  • 2 Teaspoons Italian Herb Seasoning Blend, Divided
  • 1/2 Teaspoon Granulated Garlic
  • 2 Teaspoons Paprika, Divided
  • 1/2 Cup Breadcrumbs
  • 1 Large Egg, Lightly Beaten
  • Salt and Freshly Ground Black Pepper to Taste
  • 1 Tablespoon Oil From Sundried Tomatoes
  • 1 Tablespoon Olive Oil
  • 1 Tablespoon Unsalted Butter
  • 8 Ounces Uncooked Orzo
  • 3 Cloves Garlic, Minced
  • 1 1/2 Cups Chicken Stock
  • 1 Teaspoon Red Pepper Flakes, or to Taste
  • 1 Cup Heavy Cream
  • 1/3 Cup Chopped Oil-Packed Sun-Dried Tomatoes
  • Fresh Parsley For Garnish(optional)

Directions

  1. Combine ground chicken, 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese, granulated garlic, 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning, 1 teaspoon paprika, breadcrumbs, egg in a bowl; season with salt and pepper. Mix gently with clean hands, and form into 16 meatballs.
  2. Measure out 1 tablespoon oil from the sun-dried tomatoes and heat in a large, nonstick skillet over medium heat. Add meatballs and cook until lightly browned on all sides, 8 to 10 minutes. Remove to a plate and keep warm.
  3. Add the olive oil and butter to the same skillet; heat until butter stops sizzling and add uncooked orzo. Cook, stirring, until orzo is lightly toasted and fragrant, 2 to 3 minutes. Add garlic and cook about 30 seconds, stirring constantly.
  4. Add chicken stock and stir up any browned bits. Stir in remaining Italian herb seasoning blend, paprika, and red pepper flakes. Add cream and stir. Cover and cook over medium-low heat about 8 minutes.
  5. Add sundried tomatoes and remaining Parmesan cheese. When well blended, return meatballs to the skillet and simmer until the meatballs are heated through and the orzo is tender, about 2 minutes.
  6. (OPTIONAL)Garnish with fresh parsley and serve warm.
